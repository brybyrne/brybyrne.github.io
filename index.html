agent-lab n8n

Last updated: 29 September 2026

agent-lab n8n is a personal automation app with a single user, its owner, Bryan Byrne. It is not offered to or used by anyone else. It runs on the owner's own computer.

Privacy policy

What the app accesses

The app connects to the owner's own Gmail account with read-only access (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, delete, or change email.

How the data is used

  • The app reads message headers (sender, subject, date, and Gmail's authentication result) to spot security alerts, fraud notices, and payment deadlines, using fixed rules.
  • For messages that match a rule, the sender, subject, and the reason it matched are stored on the owner's computer and sent as a notification to the owner's own private Discord channel. Nothing else from the email is sent there.
  • Email data is used only to help the owner manage his own email.

What the app does not do

  • It does not sell email data or share it with anyone other than the services named above.
  • It does not use email data for advertising.
  • It does not use email data to train AI models.

Google API data

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
